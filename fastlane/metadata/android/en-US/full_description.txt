Notes Tech: 100% local Markdown note-taking, encrypted at rest. Written in Kotlin.

<b>Radical privacy</b>
• No INTERNET permission — verifiable with <code>aapt dump permissions app.apk</code>
• No account, no telemetry, no tracker, no advertising
• SQLCipher AES-256 database, key sealed by the Android Keystore
• Multi-step panic mode: wipes keys, database and dictation model in seconds
• Optional app lock: a PIN, plus strong biometrics if you wish

<b>Per-folder vaults</b>
• Argon2id passphrase (m=64MB, t=3) or Keystore-bound PIN (Android 9+)
• AES-256-GCM per note, bound to its note (anti-swap)
• PIN auto-wipe after 5 failures
• Configurable auto-lock (5 min to never)

<b>On-device voice dictation</b>
• whisper.cpp built into the app; Whisper model imported manually
• SHA-256 verified at import and before loading
• Recordings deleted after transcription

<b>Editing</b>
• Notes open to be read (Markdown: headings, lists, links); “Edit” to write, autosave
• Long press on a note: edit, move to trash with Undo, or delete permanently
• A pastel colour per note in the lists (a vault note's only while its vault is open)
• <code>[[Title]]</code> backlinks with autocomplete + "notes linking here" panel
• Instant FTS5 search, configurable sort
• Markdown export, one note or a ZIP (YAML frontmatter, Obsidian/Logseq compatible)

<b>Five languages</b>
• English, French, German, Italian, Spanish — chosen in Settings
• Light / dark / system theme
• Privacy policy and terms in the five languages

<b>Permissions</b>
• Microphone: dictation only, asked when you turn it on
• Biometrics: app lock only, gives access to no data

<b>Open source Apache 2.0</b>
Full and verifiable source code on GitHub: gitubpatrice/notes_tech

For thinkers, therapists, students, researchers, writers and journalists who want to take sensitive or dense notes without them ever leaving their phone.
