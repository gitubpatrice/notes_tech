New: an Archive, grouped by month, in the menu next to the Trash. Archived notes leave your lists and the search, and stay one tap away. Hold a note down to select it, then others: archive, move, trash, pin, favorite, color or delete them all at once (a locked vault must be opened first). Archive is also in a note's ⋮ menu. A new note, or one opened with Edit, now gets the cursor and the keyboard at once.
