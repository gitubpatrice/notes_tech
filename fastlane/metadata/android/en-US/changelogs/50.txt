The app can finally be built from its own sources.

It depended on two internal packages by relative path, which only works
on the machine where the sibling repositories sit side by side. Anywhere
else - a plain clone, a CI run, a buildserver - resolution failed and the
app could not be built at all.

Both packages are now pulled from GitHub, pinned to an exact commit so
the result stays reproducible.

Nothing changes in the app itself.
