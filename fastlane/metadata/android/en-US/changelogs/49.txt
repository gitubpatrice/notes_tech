Fix: the PIN pad moved under your finger.

The vault sheet is anchored to the bottom of the screen, so anything
appearing above the pad shifted it: an error message moved the keys by
40 dp, and stepping to the confirmation of a new PIN dropped them by more
than a full key height. Taps were delivered, but landed in the gap
between keys.

The pad now keeps a fixed position, whatever is displayed above it.

Fixed on both vault creation and vault unlock.
