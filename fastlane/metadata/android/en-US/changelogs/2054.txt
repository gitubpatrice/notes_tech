Nothing changes in the app itself. This version exists to make the build
reproducible from the outside: the Flutter version is now pinned in the
workflows, in pubspec.yaml and in the F-Droid recipe, and the lockfile is
enforced instead of merely present.

Until now the releases were built on whatever Flutter stable happened to be,
while the committed lockfile came from a different one, so the file that is
supposed to describe what ships did not.
